Supported sites

Everywhere SillyBus AI looks for your work, and what it takes from each one.

Last updated 22 September 2026 · applies to version 0.21

All of it is read as you. The extension uses the sign-ins your Chrome already has. It never asks for a school password, never signs in on your behalf, and never sends any of it to us — everything it reads is stored on your own computer. See the privacy policy.

Where your work comes from

  • Canvas — your courses, assignments, due dates, pages, linked files and grades. Works with any school's Canvas, whatever their web address is. It is also the one site the extension can write to: if you switch handing work in on, it can upload a file to an assignment for you — off by default, and it asks every single time.
  • Veracross — your daily class schedule and your grade pages, from your school's own portal.
  • Google Docs and Drive — only the documents your teachers have already shared with you, such as syllabi and weekly homework plans, plus the PDFs those documents link to. Work assigned in a doc shows up next to work assigned in Canvas instead of getting missed.
  • AP Classroom — your assignment list and progress-check due dates, read while you have that site open. The questions inside a progress check are never read, by the extension or by the AI.
  • Albert.io — the practice sets your class has been assigned, their due dates and whether you have finished them. Question content is never read.
  • AP Central — the public Course and Exam Description for an AP subject, downloaded once and kept, so a study guide can follow the real unit framework.
  • Gmail — school mail only. The extension reads the mail feed your Chrome is already signed in to and keeps only messages from your school's domain, or messages that name one of your classes or a piece of your work; everything else is dropped before it is stored. It only ever reads — it never sends, archives, labels or deletes anything.
  • Your own calendars — any calendar feed address you paste in yourself.

The one thing that leaves your computer

The AI assistant. It is off until you paste in your own Anthropic API key, and then only what you actually ask it is sent, directly from your browser to Anthropic, billed to your account. Nothing passes through us.

Not on this list?

If your school runs on something else, say so at github.com/ElMagoCT/sillybusai-site/issues — which sources get built next is decided by what students ask for.

SillyBus AI is an independent project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by any of the companies or schools named on this page; their names appear only to say what the extension can read for you.
