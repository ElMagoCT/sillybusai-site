Setup, step by step

Opens by itself · the “finish setting up” mark
The four steps
  1. Your sign-ins — nothing to type. It uses the sites you're already signed in to in this Chrome.
  2. Connect — Canvas, your class schedule and your grades.
  3. Your classes — it finds each class's textbook, syllabus and homework doc. Check them, mark them reviewed, or skip for now.
  4. First pull — your homework lands on the board.
