All your homework on one board. It installs like any other Mac app. No Terminal, nothing to type.

Download for Mac

Free. Always the newest version. Takes about two minutes.

  1. Download it.

    Press Download for Mac. A file called SillyBusAI-Installer.dmg lands in your Downloads folder.

    DMG
    SillyBusAI-Installer.dmg

  2. Drag it to Applications.

    Double-click the file you downloaded. A window opens with Silly Bus AI inside. Drag it into your Applications folder.

    Don't open it from that window. Drag it out first. On a school Mac that won't let you use Applications, your Desktop works too.

  3. Click “Open Anyway”. Just once.

    The first time you open SillyBus, your Mac says it can't check the app. That's normal. Click Done.

    Then open System Settings → Privacy & Security. Scroll down and click Open Anyway next to Silly Bus AI. Your Mac may ask for your password or Touch ID. You never have to do this again.

    Why does my Mac ask? SillyBus is made by a student. It isn't signed with a paid Apple developer account yet, so your Mac checks with you before opening it. Every unsigned app gets this question. “Open Anyway” is Apple's own button, and it only lets this one app through.
    Privacy & Security · Security
    “Silly Bus AI” was blocked to protect your Mac.Open Anyway

  4. It sets itself up.

    The first time, SillyBus gets itself ready. This takes a couple of minutes. Then it opens and walks you through a short setup.

    After that, open it from the Dock like any other app.
