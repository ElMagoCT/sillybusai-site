Silly Bus AI Chrome extension

Your homework, in one place, on your own computer.

Silly Bus AI reads your Canvas, your schedule and the documents your teachers share, and turns them into a single board of what is actually due — inside the Chrome you already use, with the sign-ins you already have.

Nothing is uploaded. The whole app runs inside your browser. Your assignments and grades stay on your computer, and there is no account to make. See the privacy policy.

Where it is

The extension is being prepared for the Chrome Web Store. This page will carry the install link once it is published.
