What it actually does

🗂️

One board, every source

Canvas, your school's Veracross portal, syllabi and plans your teachers share as Google Docs, AP Classroom progress checks, and Albert.io practice sets — merged by due date instead of five separate tabs.

🔒

Runs on your computer

It's a Chrome extension, not a service. Your assignments and grades are stored in your own browser's storage. Nothing is uploaded, and there's no account required to use it.

✨

An AI assistant, if you want one

Ask it what to work on first, or what a rubric means. It's off until you add your own Anthropic API key, and only what you ask goes to Anthropic — billed to your account, not ours.

🎓

Honest about what it can't see

It reads with the sign-ins your Chrome already has, and says so plainly when a source can't be reached instead of guessing. AP Classroom and Albert.io questions are never read — only what's assigned and when it's due.

🚌

Built by a student, for students

Made by a high school senior who uses it on his own course load every day — not a school-vendor product built from a spec sheet.

🏫

Works beyond one school

Built first for Canvas at Brophy, extending to any Canvas school. If your school runs Canvas, it's worth trying.

How it works

  1. Install the extension. Currently shared as an unpacked build during the pilot; a Chrome Web Store listing is on the way.
  2. Point it at your school. Sign in to Canvas (and Veracross or AP Classroom, if you use them) the normal way — the extension reads with the session you already have.
  3. Get one board. Every class, ranked by what's actually due, refreshed on demand. Turn on the AI assistant if you want help prioritizing or understanding an assignment.
